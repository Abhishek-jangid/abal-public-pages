# Support

Need help with Abal, found a bug, or have an idea? Email **[abhishekk.j29@gmail.com](mailto:abhishekk.j29@gmail.com)**. Replies usually come within two working days.

It helps to include your device model, your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

## Common questions

### Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Abal has no account and no server. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).

### Will I lose my habits if I delete the app?

Yes. Your habits are stored only on your device, so deleting Abal deletes them too.

### How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Open Abal, go to **Settings**, and tap **Restore purchases** under the Premium card. If you're already a member, it's on the **Abal Premium** page in Settings. Use the same Apple Account you bought Premium with.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**, then **Abal**. You can also tap **Manage subscription** on the Abal Premium page in the app's Settings.

### How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are allowed for Abal in the **Settings** app under **Notifications > Abal**, and that Remind me is on for the habit. Reminders pause while a habit is paused. Opening Abal now and then keeps the next two weeks of reminders scheduled.

### How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap **Edit**, then **Add Widget**, and choose **Abal**.
