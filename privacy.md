# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 13 November 2026

Abal is a habit tracker for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

Abal does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. There is no account, no server, no advertising and no analytics.

## What stays on your device

Abal stores the following only on your iPhone or iPad:

- Your habits, their schedules, history, streaks and the reasons you give for them
- The quotes you choose or write
- Your settings, such as appearance and reminders
- Your focus sessions

We have no way to see this information. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it.

Abal's widgets and Live Activities read the same information from a shared space on your device (an App Group). It does not leave your device.

## Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Abal schedules them on your device. They are not sent from a server. You can turn them off at any time in Abal or in the Settings app.

## Photos

If you choose to save a share image, Abal asks for permission to **add** photos to your library. Abal cannot see, read or change the photos already there.

## Sharing

When you share an image or a day's summary, you choose where it goes using the standard iOS share sheet. Abal does not send it anywhere by itself.

## Purchases

Abal Premium is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never receive your name, email address or payment details. Abal only checks with Apple whether your device has access to Premium.

Apple's handling of your information is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children

Abal does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If Abal ever starts collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
