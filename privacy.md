# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 18 November 2026

Abal is a habit tracker for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

Abal does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. There is no account, no server, no advertising and no analytics. The only information that reaches us is feedback you choose to send.

## What stays on your device

Abal stores the following only on your iPhone or iPad:

- Your habits, their schedules, history, streaks and the reasons you give for them
- The quotes you choose or write
- Your settings, such as appearance and reminders
- Your focus sessions

We have no way to see this information. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it.

Like the rest of your device, this information can be included in your own iCloud or computer backups. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, and we can't access them.

Abal's widgets and Live Activities read the same information from a shared space on your device (an App Group). It does not leave your device.

## Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Abal schedules them on your device. They are not sent from a server. You can turn them off at any time in Abal or in the Settings app.

## Alarms

If you allow alarms, Abal sets an alarm on your device so you know when a focus session ends, even when the app is closed. The alarm is managed by iOS on your device. You can turn alarms off at any time in Abal's Settings or in the Settings app.

## Photos

If you choose to save a share image, Abal asks for permission to **add** photos to your library. Abal cannot see, read or change the photos already there.

## Sharing

When you share an image or a day's summary, you choose where it goes using the standard iOS share sheet. Abal does not send it anywhere by itself.

## Feedback

**Share your feedback** in Abal's Settings opens a form hosted by Google Forms. Sending feedback is optional. Whatever you type there, and anything Google Forms records when you submit it, is sent to us through Google and handled under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Don't include personal details you don't want to share. We use feedback only to improve Abal and never sell or share it.

If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply to you.

## Purchases

Abal Premium is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never receive your name, email address or payment details. Abal only checks with Apple whether your device has access to Premium.

**Rate Abal** opens Abal's page on the App Store, which is Apple's service.

Apple's handling of your information is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children

Abal does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through feedback or email, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If Abal ever starts collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
